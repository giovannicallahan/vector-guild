# Electricity and magnetism pack (`em`) — concept plan

Pack id `em`, name "Electricity and magnetism: fields", 40 learn + 120 practice = 160 missions (`q01`–`q40`, practice `qNNb/c/d`). Format: `docs/PACK-FORMAT.md` (every rule there applies; read "found" as "em").

**Player:** an adult who has finished Foundations, Calculus 1, Calculus 2 and Mechanics and nothing more: vectors and components, the dot product, Newton's laws, work, kinetic and potential energy, power, circular motion, simple first-order exponential growth and decay, integration by substitution and parts, and integrals over an interval. Line and surface integrals are not assumed: each is taught in the mission that first uses it (q07 surface integral as flux, q34 line integral around a loop). Words and symbols this pack teaches, each in the mission that first uses it: electric charge (q), coulomb (C), elementary charge (e), conductor, insulator, Coulomb's law, Coulomb constant (k), superposition, electric field (E), newton per coulomb (N/C), field line, charge density (λ, σ), dipole, dipole moment (p), flux (Φ), surface integral, closed surface, Gauss's law, permittivity of free space (ε₀), electric potential energy, electric potential (V), volt (V), potential difference, electron-volt (eV), equipotential, gradient, capacitor, capacitance (C), farad (F), series, parallel, dielectric, dielectric constant (κ), current (I), ampere (A), resistance (R), ohm (Ω), resistivity (ρ), Ohm's law, emf (ε), junction, loop, Kirchhoff's rules, time constant (τ), magnetic field (B), tesla (T), right-hand rule, cyclotron period, permeability of free space (μ₀), line integral (∮), Ampère's law, solenoid, magnetic flux, weber (Wb), Faraday's law, Lenz's law, motional emf, generator, inductance (L), henry (H), alternating current (ac), peak and rms, reactance (X), impedance (Z), resonance, electromagnetic wave, speed of light (c), spectrum.

**Setting:** the Kepler outpost now makes its own power. Mira runs the Power Hall at the edge of the solar field: dust that clings to panels, capacitor banks for the night, cable runs to every dome, magnet coils in the ore sorter, a wind-driven generator and the comms mast that sends light-speed signals home.

## Answers
Numbers only; a vector direction is asked as a signed component or as ±1 (Lenz's law, the right-hand rule: "enter 1 for clockwise, −1 for anticlockwise"), never as a word. **Every prompt names the unit of each answer box**, and the player types the number only. Results that would need a power of ten are asked in a stated SI prefix (nC, μF, pF, mT, μT, nm, keV) or as "the number that multiplies 10⁻¹⁵ N", so the box always takes a short decimal. Each rounded answer says how ("to 2 decimal places"); significant-figure rounding is not used. Constants are printed in the prompt: k = 8.99 × 10⁹ N·m²/C², e = 1.60 × 10⁻¹⁹ C, ε₀ = 8.85 × 10⁻¹² F/m, μ₀ = 4π × 10⁻⁷ T·m/A, c = 3.00 × 10⁸ m/s, electron and proton masses. Angles in radians. Draw values so answers are short decimals; tolerance as in `qa/pack-checks.js`.
