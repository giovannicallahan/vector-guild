# Complex analysis pack (`complex`) — concept plan

Pack id `complex`, name "Complex analysis: the plane of numbers", 40 learn + 120 practice = 160. Format: `docs/PACK-FORMAT.md` (every rule there applies; read "found" as "complex"); files split by letter a–d and practice files 1–8 exactly as `docs/CALC1-PLAN.md`.

**Player:** an adult who has finished Foundations, Calculus 1 and 2 (derivatives, integrals, series, Taylor series, the ratio test), Logic and proof and Real analysis (limits, continuity, convergence). Complex numbers, partial derivatives and line integrals are not assumed: the first is taught from scratch, the other two in the missions that need them (q13, q18). Angles are in radians; say so.
New words and symbols, each taught in the mission that first uses it: i, complex number, real part Re z, imaginary part Im z, complex plane, conjugate z̄, modulus |z|, argument arg z, principal argument Arg z, polar form, Euler's formula e^{iθ}, de Moivre's formula, nth root, root of unity, mapping w = f(z), u(x, y) and v(x, y), complex exponential, period, complex logarithm Log z, branch, principal value, complex power z^a, complex derivative f′(z), analytic, partial derivative ∂u/∂x, Cauchy–Riemann equations, sinh, cosh, harmonic, Laplace's equation, contour, parametrisation γ(t), contour integral ∫_γ f(z) dz, closed curve ∮, antiderivative, path independence, Cauchy's theorem, winding number, Cauchy's integral formula, Cauchy's inequality, entire, Liouville's theorem, mean value property, maximum modulus principle, geometric series, power series, radius of convergence, Taylor coefficient, Laurent series, annulus, singularity, removable, pole, order of a pole, essential singularity, residue Res, residue theorem, improper integral, argument principle.

**Setting:** the outpost's Wave Station, where every point of a flat plane is a single number: antenna arrays that turn signals, rotors, a flow tank round a hull model, the ring road round the field coils and a control room that has to answer integrals fast.

## Concepts (one learn mission each; Orloff section in brackets; "after" = prerequisites)

1. q01 The number i: adding and multiplying [1.3] — after none — Asked: Re and Im of z + w and of zw; varies integer parts −9 to 9.
2. q02 Conjugates and division [1.3] — after q01 — Asked: Re and Im of z ÷ w (fractions allowed) and |z|² = z z̄; varies z, w.
3. q03 Modulus and argument [1.4, 1.5] — after q02 — Asked: |z| and Arg z in radians to 2 decimal places; varies z (Pythagorean moduli).
4. q04 Euler's formula and polar form [1.6] — after q03 — Asked: Re and Im of r e^{iθ} for θ a multiple of π ÷ 6 or π ÷ 4 (2 decimal places); varies r, θ.
5. q05 Powers: de Moivre's formula [1.13, 1.6] — after q04 — Asked: Re and Im of (a + bi)ⁿ, such as (1 + i)ⁿ = 2^{n/2} e^{inπ/4}; varies n ≤ 12.
6. q06 nth roots and roots of unity [1.6] — after q05 — Asked: the number of nth roots of w, their common modulus and the real part of the one with smallest positive argument (2 decimal places); varies n, w.
7. q07 Functions as maps [1.8] — after q02 — Asked: u and v of f(z) = z² + c at a point, and Re f(z) for a second point; varies c, the points.
8. q08 The complex exponential [1.7] — after q04 — Asked: |e^{x+iy}| = e^x and Re e^{x+iy} (2 decimal places), and the y in (0, 2π] that matches e^{x+iy} after adding 2πk; varies x, y, k.
9. q09 The complex logarithm [1.11] — after q08, q03 — Asked: Re Log z = ln |z| and Im Log z = Arg z (2 decimal places); varies z.
10. q10 Complex powers [1.11] — after q09 — Asked: the principal value of i^i = e^{−π/2} (2 decimal places), Re of z^a for real a, and the number of values of z^{1/n}; varies a, z, n.
11. q11 Chapter review: the rotor log [1.7, 1.11] — after q06, q10 — Asked: a modulus, an argument and a logarithm's real part; varies.
12. q12 Limits and the complex derivative [2.3, 2.5] — after q07 — Asked: Re and Im of f′(z₀) for a polynomial f; varies coefficients, z₀.
13. q13 Cauchy–Riemann equations [2.6] — after q12 — Asked: the constant k that makes u + iv analytic (for example u = x² − ky², v = 2xy) and 1 or 0 for "this pair is analytic"; varies the pair.
14. q14 Rules of differentiation [2.5] — after q12 — Asked: Re and Im of f′(z₀) for a product, quotient or composition; varies.
15. q15 Sine, cosine, sinh and cosh [2.8, 1.12] — after q08 — Asked: cos(iy) = cosh y and |sin(x + iy)| (2 decimal places); varies x, y.
16. q16 Harmonic functions [6.2] — after q13 — Asked: the coefficient a making u = ax² + bxy + cy² + … harmonic, and the Laplacian of a non-harmonic u at a point; varies.
17. q17 Chapter review: the flow tank [2.6, 6.2] — after q14, q16 — Asked: a derivative, a Cauchy–Riemann constant and a harmonic coefficient; varies.
18. q18 Contour integrals by parametrisation [4.2] — after q12 — Asked: Re and Im of ∫_γ z dz or ∫_γ z̄ dz along a straight segment; varies the end points.
19. q19 Antiderivatives and path independence [4.3] — after q18 — Asked: Re and Im of ∫ zⁿ dz from a to b, (b^{n+1} − a^{n+1}) ÷ (n + 1); varies a, b, n.
20. q20 Powers round a circle [4.5] — after q19 — Asked: k in ∮ (z − c)ⁿ dz = kπi round |z − c| = r (2 when n = −1, else 0); varies n, c, r.
21. q21 Cauchy's theorem [4.6] — after q20, q13 — Asked: k in ∮ dz ÷ (z − a) = kπi round |z| = R (2 if |a| < R, else 0), and the integral of an analytic function round a loop (0); varies a, R.
22. q22 Holes and winding numbers [4.7] — after q21 — Asked: the winding number of a curve round a and k in ∮ dz ÷ (z − a) = kπi for a curve that winds m times; varies m, a.
23. q23 Chapter review: once round the ring road [4.5, 4.6] — after q22 — Asked: two contour integrals as multiples of πi; varies.
24. q24 Cauchy's integral formula [5.1] — after q22 — Asked: k in ∮ f(z) ÷ (z − a) dz = kπi (k = 2f(a)) for polynomial f with real coefficients and real a; varies f, a, the circle.
25. q25 The formula for derivatives [5.2] — after q24 — Asked: k in ∮ f(z) ÷ (z − a)^{n+1} dz = kπi, with k = 2f⁽ⁿ⁾(a) ÷ n!; varies f, a, n.
26. q26 Cauchy's inequality and Liouville's theorem [5.5] — after q25 — Asked: the bound n!M ÷ Rⁿ on |f⁽ⁿ⁾(a)|, and 1 or 0 for "a bounded entire function must be constant"; varies M, R, n.
27. q27 Mean value property [6.5, 5.5] — after q16, q24 — Asked: the average of u round a circle (its value at the centre) for a harmonic polynomial u; varies u, the circle.
28. q28 Maximum modulus [5.5, 6.5] — after q27 — Asked: the largest |zⁿ + c| on |z| ≤ R (Rⁿ + c for c > 0) and the argument where it happens (0); varies n, c, R.
29. q29 Chapter review: reading the field coil [5.1, 5.2] — after q26, q28 — Asked: two Cauchy-formula multiples and a bound; varies.
30. q30 Geometric series in the plane [8.1] — after q02 — Asked: Re and Im of Σ zⁿ = 1 ÷ (1 − z) for a given z with |z| < 1, such as i ÷ 2; varies z.
31. q31 Power series and radius of convergence [8.2] — after q30 — Asked: the radius R of Σ aₙzⁿ by the ratio test (for example aₙ = nᵏ ÷ bⁿ gives R = b); varies b, k.
32. q32 Taylor coefficients [8.3] — after q31, q25 — Asked: the coefficient of zᵏ in e^{az} (aᵏ ÷ k!, as a fraction) or in 1 ÷ (1 − bz) (bᵏ); varies a, b, k.
33. q33 Laurent series [8.7] — after q32 — Asked: the coefficient of z⁻¹ in zᵐ e^{a/z}, or in 1 ÷ (z(z − c)) on 0 < |z| < |c|; varies a, m, c.
34. q34 Poles and their order [8.9] — after q33 — Asked: the order of the pole at a of (z − b) ÷ ((z − a)ᵐ (z − c)), and 1 or 0 for "removable" for sin z ÷ z at 0 and similar; varies a, b, c, m.
35. q35 Residues at simple poles [9.4] — after q34 — Asked: Res = p(a) ÷ q′(a) of p(z) ÷ q(z); varies p, q with real poles.
36. q36 Residues at higher-order poles [9.4] — after q35 — Asked: the residue at a pole of order k, from g⁽ᵏ⁻¹⁾(a) ÷ (k − 1)!; varies k 2–3, g.
37. q37 The residue theorem [9.5] — after q36, q22 — Asked: k in ∮ f dz = kπi round a circle that holds some of the poles; varies the poles and the circle.
38. q38 Real integrals along the whole line [10.1] — after q37 — Asked: ∫ dx ÷ (x² + a²) over the whole line (π ÷ a) and ∫ dx ÷ ((x² + a²)(x² + b²)) (π ÷ (ab(a + b))), as multiples of π; varies a, b.
39. q39 Trigonometric integrals [10.3] — after q37 — Asked: ∫₀^{2π} dθ ÷ (a + b cos θ) = 2π ÷ √(a² − b²), as a multiple of π; varies a, b with a² − b² a square.
40. q40 Realm review and bridge to Waves [12.1] — after q38, q39 — Asked: the zeros and poles of a factored rational function inside |z| = R and the winding number of f round 0 (zeros minus poles); varies the factors, R.

Prerequisites go into each mission's `prereqs` (q01 has `[]`).

## Answers
Numbers only: a complex answer is always typed as its real part and imaginary part in two boxes; an integral equal to kπi is typed as k; a modulus, argument, logarithm or real integral is a decimal "to 2 decimal places" with tolerance 0.005; an order, count or winding number is a whole number; a property is 1 (true) or 0 (false). A function or series is never an answer: ask for a coefficient, a value at a point or a radius. Generators draw small integer coefficients, poles at integer points, angles from multiples of π ÷ 12 and Pythagorean pairs, so every answer is a short number.
