# Mechanics pack (`mech`) — concept plan

Pack id `mech`, name "Mechanics: motion and force", 40 learn + 120 practice = 160 missions (`q01`–`q40`, practice `qNNb/c/d`). Format: `docs/PACK-FORMAT.md` (every rule there applies; read "found" as "mech").

**Player:** an adult who has finished Foundations and Calculus 1 and nothing more: arithmetic, algebra, lines, quadratics, exponentials, logarithms, right-triangle and unit-circle trigonometry in radians, limits, derivatives (power, product, chain rules), antiderivatives and the definite integral by the Fundamental Theorem. No physics is assumed. Words and symbols this pack teaches, each in the mission that first uses it: SI unit, metre (m), kilogram (kg), second (s), prefix (milli-, kilo-, mega-, micro-), conversion factor, scalar, vector, magnitude, component, unit vector (î, ĵ), resultant, dot product (·), position, displacement, distance, velocity, speed, acceleration, free fall, g, projectile, range, centripetal, tangential acceleration, period, frame of reference, relative velocity, force, newton (N), net force, ΣF, mass, weight, normal force, tension, free-body diagram, Newton's three laws, inclined plane, friction, coefficient of friction (μ, static and kinetic), drag, terminal speed, work, joule (J), kinetic energy (K), work-energy theorem, power, watt (W), potential energy (U), spring constant (k), conservative force, mechanical energy, momentum (p), impulse (J), elastic and inelastic collision, centre of mass, radian as an angle of turn, angular position (θ), angular velocity (ω), angular acceleration (α), moment of inertia (I), rotational kinetic energy, torque (τ), lever arm, angular momentum (L), gravitational constant (G), orbit, orbital speed.

**Setting:** the Kepler outpost now builds and launches its own machines. Mira runs the Proving Ground on the ridge, where cargo sleds slide down ramps, cranes lift crates, a catapult throws supply pods across the crater, and flywheels and survey satellites spin overhead.

## Answers
Numbers only; a formula, a vector written as a word or a direction word is never an answer (directions become an angle in radians or a signed component). **Every prompt names the unit of each answer box** ("in metres per second"), and the player types the number only, in SI units or a named SI prefix. Each rounded answer says how ("to 2 decimal places"), with tolerance as in `qa/pack-checks.js`. Constants are given in the prompt: g = 9.8 m/s², G = 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg². Very large or small results are asked in a prefixed unit (km, kJ, minutes for an orbit) so the player never types a power of ten. Angles are in radians throughout; say so in q03 and q13. Draw parameters so most answers are whole or short decimals; signs matter (a velocity of −3 m/s).
