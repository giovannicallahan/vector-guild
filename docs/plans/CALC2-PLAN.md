# Calculus 2 pack (`calc2`) — concept plan

Pack id `calc2`, name "Calculus 2: accumulation", 40 learn missions (`q01`–`q40`) + 120 practice (`qNNb/c/d`) = 160. Format: `docs/PACK-FORMAT.md` (every rule there applies; read "found" as "calc2", and §11 as written for calc1).

**Player:** an adult who has finished Foundations and Calculus 1 and nothing more: limits, derivatives and their rules (product, quotient, chain, sin, cos, eˣ, ln x), optimisation, antiderivatives, Riemann sums, the definite integral ∫ₐᵇ f(x) dx as signed area, the Fundamental Theorem (evaluate with an antiderivative) and net change. Radians throughout; say so. New words and symbols, each taught in the mission that first uses it: accumulation function, integrand, dummy variable, average value of a function, substitution (u, du), ln|x| as an antiderivative, arctan and arcsin, area between curves, cross-section, solid of revolution, disc, washer, cylindrical shell, arc length, work (joule, J), Hooke's law, spring constant, density, mass, moment, centre of mass, centroid, x̄ and ȳ, half-life, doubling time, integration by parts, reduction of powers of sine and cosine, trigonometric substitution, partial fractions, trapezoidal rule, Simpson's rule, improper integral, converges, diverges, ∞ as a limit of integration, sequence, aₙ, term, series, Σ, partial sum Sₙ, geometric series, common ratio, telescoping series, divergence test, integral test, p-series, comparison test, limit comparison test, alternating series, remainder, ratio test, power series, centre, radius and interval of convergence, factorial n!, Taylor polynomial, Taylor series, Maclaurin series, Taylor remainder, parametric equations, parameter.

**Setting:** the Kepler outpost is building to last: the Reservoir from Calculus 1 now feeds a hull foundry, cargo cranes, a deep-space signal array and a navigation room. Mira is still the mentor. Objects are tanks and pipes, hull plates and domes, crane cables and springs, fuel decaying in storage, signal echoes that fade, and star-compass tables.

## Concepts (one learn mission each; OpenStax Calculus Volume 2 section in brackets; prerequisites after the arrow)

1. q01 Net change from a rate [1.4] ← [] — Asked: the net change and the final amount (litres in a tank fed at r(t) L/min). Varies: rate coefficients, interval, starting amount.
2. q02 Accumulation functions: the derivative of ∫ₐˣ f(t) dt [1.3] ← q01 — Asked: F(b) and F′(b). Varies: polynomial f, a, b.
3. q03 Average value of a function on an interval [1.3] ← q01 — Asked: the average value, then the input where f takes it. Varies: f, interval.
4. q04 Substitution, including changing the limits [1.5] ← q02 — Asked: the new limits in u, then the value of the integral. Varies: inner function and powers.
5. q05 Integrals that give ln|x| and eˣ [1.6] ← q04 — Asked: an integral's value to 3 decimal places. Varies: coefficients, limits.
6. q06 Integrals that give arctan and arcsin [1.7] ← q04 — Asked: the value in radians to 3 decimal places. Varies: a in 1/(a² + x²), limits.
7. q07 Area between two curves [2.1] ← q01 — Asked: the two crossing x-values, then the area. Varies: line and parabola coefficients.
8. q08 Curves that swap places; slicing along y [2.1] ← q07 — Asked: the total area. Varies: crossing points.
9. q09 Volume by slicing with known cross-sections [2.2] ← q07 — Asked: the volume in m³. Varies: base curve, square or triangle slices.
10. q10 Discs and washers [2.2] ← q09 — Asked: the volume as k·π: enter k. Varies: curves, axis.
11. q11 Chapter review: areas and solids mixed [2.1, 2.2] ← q08, q10 — Asked: an area and a volume (k of k·π). Varies: as q08 and q10.
12. q12 Cylindrical shells [2.3] ← q10 — Asked: k in k·π. Varies: curve, interval, axis x = c.
13. q13 Arc length of a curve [2.4] ← q04 — Asked: the length to 2 decimal places. Varies: curves chosen to give clean lengths, such as y = a·x^(3/2), and the interval.
14. q14 Work done by a varying force; springs [2.5] ← q01 — Asked: the spring constant, then the work in joules. Varies: stretch, force reading.
15. q15 Pumping water out of a tank [2.5] ← q14, q09 — Asked: work in kJ to 1 decimal place (ρ = 1000 kg/m³, g = 9.8 m/s²). Varies: tank shape and size, outlet height.
16. q16 Mass, moments and centre of mass [2.6] ← q07 — Asked: the mass of a rod of varying density, then x̄ (or x̄ and ȳ of a plate). Varies: density, region.
17. q17 Chapter review: shells, work and balance [2.3, 2.5] ← q12, q15 — Asked: two of volume, work, x̄. Varies: as above.
18. q18 Integration by parts [3.1] ← q04 — Asked: the value to 3 decimal places (x·eˣ, x·sin x, ln x). Varies: coefficients, limits.
19. q19 Powers of sine and cosine [3.2] ← q04 — Asked: an exact value as a fraction (or k of k·π). Varies: powers, limits that are multiples of π/2.
20. q20 Trigonometric substitution [3.3] ← q19 — Asked: area of part of a circular plate as k·π, or a value to 3 decimal places. Varies: radius, limits.
21. q21 Partial fractions [3.4] ← q05 — Asked: the two numerators A and B, then the integral to 3 decimal places. Varies: the two linear factors.
22. q22 Trapezoidal and Simpson's rules [3.6] ← q01 — Asked: both estimates to 3 decimal places from a table of readings. Varies: readings, n.
23. q23 Chapter review: choosing a technique [3.5] ← q18, q21 — Asked: the index (1–4) of the right technique, then the value. Varies: integrand family.
24. q24 Improper integrals over an endless interval [3.7] ← q05 — Asked: 1 if it converges, 0 if not, then its value. Varies: p in 1/xᵖ, e^(−kx).
25. q25 Improper integrals where the integrand blows up [3.7] ← q24 — Asked: 1 or 0, then the value. Varies: p, the endpoint.
26. q26 Exponential growth and decay; half-life [2.8] ← q05 — Asked: the rate constant k to 4 decimal places, then a time to 2 decimal places. Varies: half-life, amounts.
27. q27 Sequences and their limits [5.1] ← q24 — Asked: a given term aₙ, then the limit. Varies: rational and exponential formulas.
28. q28 Geometric series [5.2] ← q27 — Asked: the common ratio, then the sum. Varies: first term, ratio (|r| < 1, fractions).
29. q29 Chapter review: the infinite so far [3.7, 5.1, 5.2] ← q25, q28 — Asked: a value of an improper integral and a geometric sum. Varies: as above.
30. q30 Partial sums, telescoping series, the divergence test [5.2, 5.3] ← q28 — Asked: S₁₀ and the sum (or 0 when the divergence test says it diverges). Varies: telescoping pair.
31. q31 The integral test and p-series [5.3] ← q30, q24 — Asked: 1 or 0, then the integral bound on the tail after N terms. Varies: p, N.
32. q32 Comparison and limit comparison tests [5.4] ← q31 — Asked: the limit of aₙ ÷ bₙ, then 1 or 0. Varies: rational terms.
33. q33 Alternating series and the error bound [5.5] ← q30 — Asked: the smallest number of terms that makes the error below a given size. Varies: 1/n, 1/n², tolerance.
34. q34 Ratio and root tests [5.6] ← q32 — Asked: the limit L as a fraction, then 1 or 0. Varies: bases, factorials.
35. q35 Power series: radius and interval of convergence [6.1] ← q34 — Asked: the centre, then the radius. Varies: centre, scale.
36. q36 Taylor polynomials [6.3] ← q35 — Asked: a coefficient, then the polynomial's value at a point to 4 decimal places. Varies: function, centre, degree.
37. q37 Maclaurin series of eˣ, sin x, cos x and 1/(1 − x) [6.3] ← q36 — Asked: the coefficient of xⁿ as a fraction. Varies: function, n, a scaled input.
38. q38 Taylor's remainder: how good is the estimate [6.3] ← q36, q33 — Asked: the smallest degree that guarantees a given accuracy. Varies: point, accuracy.
39. q39 Integrating a series to estimate an integral [6.4] ← q37 — Asked: the estimate to 4 decimal places. Varies: e^(−x²) or sin(x)/x, limit.
40. q40 Realm review and bridge to Calculus 3: a path given by x(t) and y(t) [7.1, 7.2] ← q13, q39 — Asked: the slope dy/dx at a time, then the length of the path. Varies: parametric polynomials, times.

## Answers

Numbers only; a function is never an answer. Volumes with π ask for the multiple k of π. Convergence is typed as 1 (converges) or 0 (diverges), always followed by a number (a sum, a bound, a limit) so guessing cannot pass. Rounded answers say how to round, with tolerance as in §11. Generators draw coefficients so crossing points and limits are whole numbers or simple fractions.
